Lab 10 - trace: answers

1. Why only read, and not open or close, in "trace 32 grep hello README"?
   The mask is a bit set: bit N on means "print call number N". 32 = 2^5 and SYS_read is 5, so only read is on. open (15) and close (21) are not in the mask, so they are not printed (they still happen).

2. Which line of Part 2 lets three pids print in forkforkfork?
   np->trace_mask = p->trace_mask;   (kernel/proc.c, in fork)
   Every child copies the mask of its parent, so all the descendants are traced as well.

3. Why is the trace line printed between "hi" and the new line in "trace 65536 echo hi"?
   echo calls write twice: once for "hi" (2 bytes) and once for the "\n" (1 byte). The kernel prints the trace line after each call has finished, so "69: syscall write -> 2" comes right after "hi" and before the newline is written; the second trace line (-> 1) comes after the newline.

4. Which mask traces open (15) and close (21), and nothing else?
   2^15 + 2^21 = 32768 + 2097152 = 2129920
